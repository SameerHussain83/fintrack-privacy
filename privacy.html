Privacy Policy

FinTrack · Last updated: 15 November 2026

FinTrack is a personal finance app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

1. Information stored by the app

The information you enter in FinTrack – such as expenses, room members' names and phone numbers, payments, credit card names and the last four digits of cards, bills, account balances and settings – is stored only on your own device.

FinTrack never asks for or stores full card numbers, CVV codes, bank passwords or PINs.

2. Information we collect

None. The developer of FinTrack does not run any server and does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking in the app.

3. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive, FinTrack uses Google Sign-In to request the drive.file permission. With this permission FinTrack can only see and manage the files it creates itself. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.

FinTrack uses this permission only to save backup copies of your FinTrack data into a folder named “FinTrack Backups” in your own Google Drive, and to read those backups back when you choose to restore. The backups are not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

FinTrack’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in FinTrack (Settings → Backup → Disconnect Google Drive) and remove FinTrack’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Other services the app uses

  • Exchange rates: to show amounts in a second currency, FinTrack downloads public exchange rates from open.er-api.com. No personal data is sent.
  • WhatsApp and sharing: statements are only sent when you tap Share or WhatsApp, to the person you choose.
  • Receipt scanning and fingerprint unlock run entirely on your device.

5. Your control over your data

You can edit or delete any entry in the app. Uninstalling FinTrack, or clearing its storage, deletes the data on your device. Backup files you saved (on your phone or in your Google Drive) remain under your control and can be deleted by you at any time.

6. Children

FinTrack is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
