FinTrack

A personal finance app for Android

FinTrack helps you keep track of your money in one place:

  • Shared room expenses, split between roommates, with monthly statements
  • Personal spending and monthly budget
  • Credit cards, bills and subscriptions with payment reminders
  • Money lent and borrowed
  • Cash and bank account balances

All data is stored on your own phone. You can optionally back it up to your own Google Drive.
